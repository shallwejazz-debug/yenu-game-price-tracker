# Why YEONU DEAL is archived

YEONU DEAL is being frozen before a possible root-domain WordPress migration. This is a service pause, not a deletion.

The central dependency is Naver Shopping Search/API access and the availability of reliable price/provider data. The service can be resumed when a compliant Naver collection method, changed API policy, or a suitable replacement price-data provider is available.

Archive and restoration work is limited to YEONU DEAL resources. The separate YEONU LAB service remains independent.
